Beginner guides

Three realistic ways to get started online

Pick a path that matches your current skills and equipment. Build a sample before spending money.

📱

Offer a simple digital service

Practise making flyers, social captions, short-video edits, or product descriptions. Create three samples, then show them to potential customers and agree on the price before starting.

📚

Create a digital product

Make an original planner, checklist, template, or short guide that solves one clear problem. Check platform fees, payout support in your country, and licensing before listing it.

🎥

Grow a content page

Choose one topic, publish useful posts consistently, and learn from audience response. Monetisation is not guaranteed and platform eligibility rules apply.

Simple workflow

Your first three steps

Choose one audience

For example, small shops that need promotional flyers or creators who need short-video captions.

Make a sample

Use a free tool to create one example. Keep it original and do not use copyrighted work without permission.

Ask for feedback

Show the sample to a few people. Improve it before offering the service or product for sale.
